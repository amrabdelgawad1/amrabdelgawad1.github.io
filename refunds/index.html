Refunds

Refund policy

Last updated November 18, 2026. When you can get your money back for a course or a service, and how to ask.

Courses

  • If a course is not right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of buying it.
  • The refund is given in full if you finished less than 30% of the lessons and no final certificate was issued.
  • After 14 days, after 30% of the lessons, or once the final certificate is issued, the course cannot be refunded.
  • Course bonuses (calls, reviews, templates) are part of the course and are not refunded on their own.

How to ask

Email amr@amrabdelgawad.site from the email you bought with and say which course. You get an answer within 3 working days. Approved refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, through the same payment service. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Access after a refund

When a refund is made, access to that course closes. Your progress is kept for 30 days in case you buy it again.

Calling teams and services

Work for clients (calling teams, lead management) is billed by invoice for hours and work already done, as the client agreement says. Hours already worked are not refunded. If something went wrong with the work, email amr@amrabdelgawad.site and it will be put right first.

Payment problems

If you were charged twice or charged by mistake, email amr@amrabdelgawad.site and the extra charge is refunded in full, at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. The policy that applied on the day you paid is the one used for your purchase.
