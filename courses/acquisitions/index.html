Questions

Who is this for?

Anyone who wants to work in acquisitions for a real estate investor, or a cold caller or lead manager who wants to move up to the acquisitions seat. No experience is needed; Module 1 starts with the words and the basics.

I want to wholesale on my own. Is this for me?

Yes, for the hardest part. Acquisitions (finding motivated sellers, running the numbers and getting the contract signed) is where most new wholesalers struggle, and this course covers it in full. Finding cash buyers and selling the deal is covered as an overview; a full dispositions course is planned for later.
